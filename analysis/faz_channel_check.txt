Reading: the FAZ is segmented as well in every run (threshold Dice 0.93; the FAZ channel learns within the first epochs in all runs, 0.88 by epoch 3-6).
In seeds 2 and 3 the background channel ALSO outputs probability 1.0 inside the FAZ; the channels are trained with independent sigmoids and per-channel Dice,
nothing forces the background channel to be 0 on the few thousand FAZ pixels (a negligible part of its Dice), so the argmax between 0.95 (FAZ) and 1.00
(background) decides the pixel and often goes to the background. In seed 1 and in the Mac run the background channel learned to be low inside the FAZ.
Which of the two happens depends on the training seed (the three Kaggle runs differ only in the seed; all five folds of a run behave alike because
the folds of a run share the seed). Not a platform effect and not slow learning. The vessel Dice does not suffer (vessel channel 0.00 inside the FAZ).
Possible remedies (not tried; no new experiments): a mutual-exclusion term between FAZ and background (the existing --exclusion handles FAZ/vessel only),
a softmax over the three channels, or reporting the thresholded FAZ Dice. The classifier is not affected (it uses decoder features, not the argmax map).
