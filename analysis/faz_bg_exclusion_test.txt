Reading: the option removes the diagnosed error (the background channel no longer claims the FAZ: recall of the argmax FAZ rises from 0.83-0.88 to 0.99, and the argmax FAZ
appears from the first epochs instead of after 12-24), but it creates another one: about 70-80 % of the new argmax false positives (35k-52k pixels over 73 scans) have a FAZ probability
below 0.5, i.e. the background probability also falls just outside the FAZ and the FAZ channel wins the argmax with a low probability. Net effect on the final argmax FAZ Dice: +0.006
(seed 2) and -0.002 (seed 3), i.e. none; the thresholded FAZ Dice is unchanged (+0.004) or lower (-0.017). The pre-set criterion 2 is therefore not met and the option is NOT a fix for
the argmax FAZ Dice; it trades a recall error for a precision error. Not tried (no tuning, one weight, two seeds, one fold): a smaller weight, a penalty confined to the FAZ boundary,
a softmax over the three channels, or reporting the thresholded FAZ Dice (0.93 in every run), which the repository already does. Classification on fold 0 (not a claim): balanced accuracy
0.716 -> 0.716 (seed 2), 0.704 -> 0.784 (seed 3); one fold, within the run-to-run noise.
